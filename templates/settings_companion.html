Companion app

Pair the community-built iOS companion so it can list displays and dashboards and send from your phone. Editing and administration stay here in the web UI.

Get the app

The app is in public beta on TestFlight. Scan the code with an iPhone, or open the link on the device you want to pair.

Join the TestFlight beta

Requires the TestFlight app from the App Store. Beta builds expire, so you may need to update it from TestFlight now and then. Issue a pairing code below once it's installed.

Pair a phone

Each paired app gets its own token you can revoke here at any time.

Issue a code below, open the app, and enter it to pair. Codes are single-use and expire in 10 minutes.
