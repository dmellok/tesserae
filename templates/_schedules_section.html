Timed sends

A timed send is a deck that puts one dashboard on its displays by the clock, on an interval or at a daily time. To cycle several dashboards automatically, use a timer cycle; to flip between them by hand, use a deck with links. Interval sends respect the day-of-week mask and the time-of-day window; daily sends respect the mask only.
