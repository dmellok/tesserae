Firmware

Update device firmware over the air. Queue a device and it is offered the signed release on its next heartbeat; the device fetches and verifies the image itself, and this page never touches the firmware bytes. Visionect frames are the exception: they run Visionect's own firmware, which Tesserae downloads from Visionect and sends to the frame when you ask.
